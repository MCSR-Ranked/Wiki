Fast Looting is a fundamental technique that saves time in speedruns. Every beginner should learn it as fast as possible. There are two main types of fast looting: 4 input and 3 input. Fast looting with 3 input should only be used at the beginning of a match or when your hotbar slots are free.

## How to Fast Loot: 4 input
1. Hold down shift. Do not let go of shift for the entire duration of the loot.
2. Click any hotkey. This can be a hotbar slot (1-9), or your offhand. For beginners, offhand is recommended because it does not mess with your hotbar setup.
3. Click your left mouse button (or whichever button you have set to "Attack/Destroy")
4. Click the same hotkey again.
5. Click the left mouse button again.

You must do the sequence above as fast as possible, minimizing the time between inputs. If done correctly, the item you were hovering over at the beginning should move to your inventory, along with its whole stack.
## How to Fast Loot: 3 input
1. Hold down shift. Do not let go of shift for the entire duration of the loot.
2. Left click
3. Hotkey to your furthest available hotbar slot
4. Left click

Again, you must do the sequence as fast as possible. Otherwise, it won't work.

***It is important to note that when looting multiple items with 3 input, you must roll down your hotbar slots. The first item should be used with your last available hotkey (when starting a speedrun, this is always your 9th slot), and each consecutive item should be hotkeyed to the previous slot (2nd item should go to slot 8, etc.). This means you cannot use 3 input looting with your offhand. It is very unlikely that you will be able to use 3 input looting in any split other than the overworld.***
